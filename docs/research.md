# Research notes: Diligent Entities and entity management software

Checked 11 October 2026.

## What Diligent Entities charges

- Diligent publishes no price. The Entities product page and the Diligent pricing page ask buyers to request pricing; the pricing page describes "a package tailored to your organization's size and stage of growth" and tiered pricing. Sources: https://www.diligent.com/products/entities, https://www.diligent.com/pricing
- Buyer-reported quotes collected by software marketplaces sit in the tens of thousands of US dollars a year for a small entity portfolio and more for large ones. These are third-party figures and are not used on the page.

## How Diligent Entities exports

- Quick Reports (Companies, Individuals, All Entities, Appointments, Shareholders and others) search the records and export the results as an Excel file. Appointments has over 100 data columns, Individuals over 50 (including the Director Identification Number), Companies over 200. Source: Diligent help centre, Quick Reports (help.diligentoneplatform.com, entities/quick-reports).
- Data Exchange Manager offers Excel extracts as well.

## The rivals New Zealand and Australian practices meet

- BGL CAS 360: company, trust and AML management software, A$19 excluding GST per company per year, minimum 25 companies, sold in lots of 5 after that, unlimited users. Source: https://www.bglcorp.com/pricing/ (checked 11 October 2026).
- Athennian: entity management, quoted privately.
